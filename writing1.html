What is a ritual, practice, or routine in your life that is important for your psychological wellbeing and/or fulfillment? Why?

How does this ritual or behavior contribute to your life? What aspect of it makes it valuable to you? What can it teach us about the nature or ingredients of human fulfillment more broadly?


My most important ritual is to walk from my architecture studio back to my dorm on foot, and to have a 30mins snack time no matter when of the day. This routine helps clear up my mind from all the repetitive iterating moments in my design processes and prepares me for bed time. Normally this will be 2am or 3am late night, and neither school shuttles nor golf cars are running. Under this circumstance, my friend prefers to call an uber considering the late night cold and the safety concerns. This shortens the trip back to dorm from 15 mins to 5 mins and completely cut off my thinking process, which is a transitional moment that helps me recognize my own progress each day.

I think human fulfillment largely consists of self-achievement and a certain satisfaction from all the rituals we will carry on everyday (like sleeping, eating, exercising). My specific ritual is like a “liminal space” in between my work everyday as a designer and my ordinary life as a human.
