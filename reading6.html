This article uses the tool hammer to illustrate the moral aspect of technologies really well. Technologies are often perceived as neutral and harmless, despite the making and consuming of technologies involves so many resources and people. I've read an article about techonologies as prosthetics. Their high involvement to human activities designates that they reshape who we are, and thus, couldn't be neutral.

Take the use of AI as an example. When we use AI to generate images, we violate the copy rights of millions of referrence photos and artworks that have been feed into AIs. And if we chat with AI, there comes a loss of autonomy and privacy. Behind the screen tech companies also collect your responses to their data base. Even false information can be feed to AIs and disturbs the academic community. As for the most direct harm of using AI, only one ChatGPT interaction consumes a 500ml bottle of water. The making and using of those prosthetics are more profound than we ever thought of.
