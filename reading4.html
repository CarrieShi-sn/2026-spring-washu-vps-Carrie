- Website allows us to create both the works (objects) and the world (architecture) - this cues the creators to think of curations and the very fundamental logic behind the works (a thing that in turn encourages creativity. Plus curating used to be feasible only during exhibitions, but now everyone can experience with it using websites)

- Websites should be unfinished - that’s the beauty of this digital space. The iterating process of websites can be thought-provoking.

- I want my website to be a plant growing from a puddle - this will reflect my thinking process - usually I have a bunch of messy, creative ideas at the beginning, and then each idea grows to a specific branch——something that will grow by themselves and continue having more branches (also coordinating with my different fields of interests)
